Opening Ceremony

🎁 Surprise Challenge

Revealed ONLY at the opening — adapt fast!

➕
Surprise Task
An extra object or mission added to the field.

Example: transport a rotten fruit to the compost area.

→ Optional to complete, but earns bonus points.
→ Object usually must stay on the field.
VS
🔄
Surprise Rule
A small change to the existing challenge rules.

Example: colours of objects are switched — yellow goes to market, red to compost.

→ Mandatory to follow — forces re-programming.
→ May or may not award extra points.
⚡ Both types are presented at the opening ceremony and apply to all rounds throughout the day.
Practice Time

Practice Time Rules

What you can & cannot do during practice

✅
Allowed
Modify code and adjust programs
Change mechanics on the robot
Make test runs — queue with robot (controller included)
Calibrate sensors on official tables (ask judge)
Assemble robot (only at start of 1st practice)
🚫
Not Allowed
Bring laptops to the competition table
Bring own mats to the team area
Calibrate sensors right before a run (must be during practice)
Coaches entering team area to give instructions
Any wireless communication on during runs
🔧 Once practice time ends → judges check robots → tables prepared for next round (including randomization)
Quarantine

🔒 Quarantine & Robot Check

What happens before each official round

  • 📍
    Robot must be placed in the quarantine / robot parking area before practice time ends — robot must be ready to go.
  • 🔘
    Only one further press of the start button is allowed after quarantine. All wireless communication must be OFF.
  • 🔍
    Judges inspect the robot — check size, regulations, Bluetooth off, etc. Violations get a 3-minute fix window. No new programs allowed during those 3 minutes.
  • 📋
    Technical Summary is judged at the first quarantine only — those points apply for the entire day.
  • ⚠️
    If the violation can't be fixed in time → team is disqualified for that attempt (0 points, 120 sec).
Robot Attempt

The 2-Minute Run

2
Minutes Max
1

0
Laptops at Table
  • ⏱️
    Time starts when judge signals. Stops at 2 min, or if run ends early.
  • 🤚
    Run ends if: team member touches robot or objects, robot leaves the table, team shouts "STOP", or violation occurs.
  • ✍️
    Scores are signed off by team after each run. No complaints after signing. No video/photo proofs accepted.
  • 🔧
    Physical adjustments allowed in start area — but NO data entry, NO sensor calibration, NO arm repositioning to input info.
Technical Summary

📄 Technical Summary

Judged once — counts all day

📋
What It Is
A one-page document summarising your robot's key facts — helps judges understand your robot.

Checked during the first quarantine only. Those points are locked in for the rest of the day.
🎯
How It's Scored
Up to 5 bonus points added to your total score.

Judges only check whether it was completed — quality doesn't matter, just fill it in!

Appears as a separate row on the scoresheet between Max Score and Total Score.
💡 Easy 5 points — make sure your technical summary is ready before the first quarantine!

Summary

Key Reminders

Don't let these catch you off guard

⚡
Surprise = Opening Ceremony
You won't know the Surprise Task or Rule until the day itself. Have a backup plan ready and stay flexible with your code.
📋
Technical Summary = Easy Points
5 free points if you bring a completed summary. Checked at first quarantine only — don't forget it!
🔒
Quarantine = Final State
Robot must be fully ready before quarantine. No new code, no wireless. Only one start-button press allowed after.
✍️
Sign = Final
Once you sign the scoresheet, no disputes are possible. Check your score carefully before signing each run.
